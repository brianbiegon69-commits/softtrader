Strategy library

Plain-English notes on common approaches, for learning. On synthetic indices the price moves are random by design, so no strategy guarantees a profit, and every one of them can lose money.

Digits

Even / Odd

You predict whether the last digit of the next tick will be even or odd. The two outcomes are roughly equally likely, so a pattern in recent digits does not give you an edge.

  • Use a small, fixed stake
  • Decide how many trades you will take before you start
  • Stop at your daily loss limit
Digits

Over / Under

You pick a barrier digit and predict whether the last digit will be over or under it. A barrier that is less likely to hit pays more, and one that is more likely to hit pays less, so the odds are priced in.

  • Check the payout against the real probability
  • Higher payout means lower chance, not free money
  • Keep the stake the same on every trade
Direction

Rise / Fall

You predict whether the price will finish higher or lower after a set duration. Following a recent trend can feel right, but short-term moves on synthetic indices are not predictable.

  • Practise on a demo account first
  • Write your entry rule down before trading
  • Do not change the rule in the middle of a losing run
Risk

Risk management rules

These matter more than any entry signal, because they limit how much you can lose.

  • Risk only a small part of your balance per trade (many traders use 1–2%)
  • Set a daily loss limit and stop when you reach it
  • Stop after a set number of losses in a row
  • Never increase your stake to win back losses

Doubling the stake after a loss (martingale) can wipe out an account in a short losing streak.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is Pulsedesk part of Deriv?

No. Pulsedesk is an independent tool that connects to your own Deriv account using Deriv's official login. Deriv does not operate or endorse it.

Does it cost anything?

Pulsedesk itself is free to use. Trading on Deriv has its own fees and risks, which are set by Deriv.

Can I lose money?

Yes. Trading derivatives is risky and you can lose your entire stake. Only trade with money you can afford to lose.

Do you guarantee profits?

No, and nobody can. Nothing on this site is financial advice or a promise of results.
