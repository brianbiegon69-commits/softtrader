Questions

Straight answers.

Is Pulsedesk part of Deriv?

No. Pulsedesk is an independent tool that connects to your own Deriv account using Deriv's official login. Deriv does not operate or endorse it.

Does it cost anything?

Pulsedesk itself is free to use. Trading on Deriv has its own fees and risks, which are set by Deriv.

Can I lose money?

Yes. Trading derivatives is risky and you can lose your entire stake. Only trade with money you can afford to lose.

Do you guarantee profits?

No, and nobody can. Nothing on this site is financial advice or a promise of results.
